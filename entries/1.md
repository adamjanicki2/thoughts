# Argue with a wall

The purpose of this first thought is pretty self-evident by the title and thumbnail. My thesis is: **2011 is the greatest year in the history of music**. Seriously, I had this revelation when I was mixing some stuff on Spotify, and noticed how many of the songs that were on my playlist were released in 2011. Included in this is my most favorite album of all time, and a few of my favorite songs of all time. Let's check them out below.

# Mylo Xyloto

![Mylo Xyloto](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RvV0ugVkOvU)

This is my favorite album of all time, straight up. And **Paradise** might be my favorite song of all time _(I'm not entirely decided)_. What's unique about this album is the way all the songs transition into one another. That means, if you listen to the whole album straight through, it almost sounds like one continuous song. I'll admit, some of them were better transitions than others. I think the best transition is without a doubt between the first two songs: the namesake **Mylo Xyloto** and **Hurts like Heaven**.

# EDM Renaissance

![Levels](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_ovdm2yX4MA)

2011 has the advantage of being right in the middle of the EDM renaissance. So many new school artists were emerging at the beginning of the 2010s. Names like Alesso, David Guetta, Calvin Harris, and the king, Avicii. **Levels** is probably the reason why I can't make up my mind on whether **Paradise** is my favorite song of all time.

But Avicii's bangers of the year go even deeper than that. He's my favorite artist of all time, so let me help you out with some deeper cuts:

- [Fade Into Darkness](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-yg3dLEDWqs)
- [Sunshine](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eO_q3ldG6To)
- [Derezzed](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FPohMBj0aKE) _(a little movie interlude had to be included in my first Thought; I think this is my most listened to song on Spotify to date)_

In fact, I just went and checked my all-time Spotify leaderboard, and here's how it stacks up; the top 3 songs are all from 2011!

1. Derezzed _(Avicii & Daft Punk)_
2. Levels _(Avicii)_
3. Sunshine _(Avicii & David Guetta)_

So, Avicii naturally takes the lead given that he's my favorite artist of all time. But, I said EDM Renaissance for a reason! Here are some other incredible songs from the golden era, in no particular order!

- [Feel So Close](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dGghkjpNCQ8) _(Calvin Harris)_
- [Titanium](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JRfuAukYTKg) _(David Guetta & Sia)_
- [Save the World](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BXpdmKELE1k) _(Swedish House Mafia)_
- [We Found Love](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tg00YEETFzg) _(Calvin Harris & Rihanna)_

# Popping off

![Midnight City](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dX3k_QDnzHE)

I don't really know how to describe these last few songs; they're borderline EDM, but not really; I'd classify them more as pop. But these last few really round out the selection of songs I've presented to you today. These are the songs that really solidify my opinion. They make me feel, that, without a doubt, if I had to go the rest of my life only listening to music from a single year, 2011 wins hands down.

**Lights** by Ellie Goulding is an interesting one. It's one of the most memory invoking songs I can think of. Seriously, when I hear it, I'm instantly transported back to being a 5th grader. The vocals combined with the low intensity borderline electronic music was likely one of my gateways into EDM.

And to cap us off: **Midnight City**. I wasn't sure whether to include this in the EDM section. It's not quite EDM. It's something like synthpop. This is certainly in my top 10 songs of all time. Maybe even top 5. I'm not sure. But what I can say for certain is it has the best vibes of any song I've ever heard. It's one of the songs I can visualize. I can feel it. Summertime, listening to it in the car, a little humid, but not overbearingly hot. Windows down, and those pulsing waves of the synth flooding the roads beyond.
